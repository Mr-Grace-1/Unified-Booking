# 🎯 Who Can Use UnifiedBook - Complete Guide

## 📋 Overview

UnifiedBook is a **multi-tenant, role-based booking management system** designed for businesses of all sizes that need to manage appointments, services, and customer bookings.

1. **Pure E-commerce** (no services/appointments)
   - Better options: Shopify, WooCommerce

2. **Simple Contact Forms** (no booking needed)
   - Better options: Contact Form 7, Typeform

3. **Internal Team Management Only** (no customer bookings)
   - Better options: Asana, Trello, Monday.com

4. **Inventory-Only Management** (no services)
   - Better options: TradeGecko, Cin7

5. **Pure Point-of-Sale** (no appointment scheduling)
   - Better options: Square POS, Toast

6. **Complex Project Management** (not service booking)
   - Better options: Jira, Basecamp
