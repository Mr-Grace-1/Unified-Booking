## 🚀 How to Use the Preview

### 1. **First Load - Login Page**
When you open the preview, you'll see the **animated login page** with:
- Beautiful gradient background with floating orbs
- Email and password fields
- "Sign In" button
- Link to switch to signup mode

### 2. **Login with Demo Credentials**
```
Email: admin@demo.com
Password: demo123
```

Click "Sign In" and watch the smooth loading animation, then you'll be redirected to the main dashboard.

### 3. **Explore the Dashboard**
After login, you'll see:
- **Stats cards** with today's bookings, revenue, customers, staff
- **Quick action buttons** for booking, calendar, customers
- **Today's schedule** with animated booking cards
- **Recent activity** feed
- **Status summary** (pending, confirmed, completion rate)
- **Tenant Information card** showing:
  - Tenant name: "Demo Company"
  - Plan: Professional
  - Data Region: US East (Virginia) 🇺🇸
  - Compliance badges: SOC2, HIPAA
  - Session timeout: 60 minutes
  - Data retention: 365 days
  - Password policy: Standard
  - Data sovereignty notice

### 4. **User Menu**
Click your avatar in the top-right header to see:
- Your name: "Sarah Chen"
- Your email: admin@demo.com
- Your role: admin
- **Sign Out** button

### 5. **Test the Signup Flow**
1. Click "Sign Out" from the user menu
2. On the login page, click "Sign up"
3. Fill in:
   - Full Name: Your name
   - Email: your@email.com
   - Password: Create a password (watch the strength indicator!)
4. Click "Create Account"
5. You'll enter the **4-step onboarding wizard**

### 6. **Complete Onboarding**

**Step 1: Business Info**
- Enter business name
- Select business type (8 options with icons)
- Click "Next"

**Step 2: Data Region**
- Choose from 4 regions:
  - 🇺🇸 US East (Virginia) - SOC2, HIPAA
  - 🇺🇸 US West (Oregon) - SOC2, HIPAA
  - 🇪🇺 EU West (Ireland) - GDPR, SOC2
  - 🇸🇬 Asia Pacific (Singapore) - PDPA, SOC2
- See compliance badges
- Read data sovereignty notice
- Click "Next"

**Step 3: Team Setup (Optional)**
- Add team members (or skip)
- Assign roles (Admin/Manager/Staff)
- Click "Next"

**Step 4: Preferences**
- Toggle notifications
- Toggle marketing updates
- Toggle analytics
- Click "Complete Setup"

### 7. **Explore All Features**
After onboarding, you have full access to:
- 📅 **Bookings** - View and manage all bookings
- ➕ **New Booking** - Create new bookings with multi-step wizard
- 📆 **Calendar** - Day and month views
- 🛍️ **Services** - Browse all services with real images
- 👥 **Customers** - Customer CRM with profiles
- 👨‍💼 **Staff** - Team management
- 📍 **Locations** - Multi-location management
- 🔌 **Integrations** - View connected services with real icons
- 📊 **Analytics** - Business insights with animated charts
